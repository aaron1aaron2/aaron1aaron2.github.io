Horus multi-camera person tracking

How do you recognize the same person after a track breaks? I integrated appearance matching into the team's tracking system.

  • FastReID
  • Cosine similarity
  • Person ReID
Role
Responsible for person ReID, combining FastReID appearance features, multiple samples and two-threshold decisions with queued matching and tracking-record updates.
Results and status

Context and objectives

Turning, occlusion or leaving and re-entering a camera view can change a person's tracking ID, while faces may not be visible. The team combined tracking, face recognition and ReID to reconnect separate track segments.

System architecture and workflow

Architecture overview

Inputs
Multi-camera track segments and images of people across times and angles.
Process
I integrated FastReID features, comparing similarity and support across multiple samples and connecting the results to tracking records through a queue.
Outputs
Team multi-camera tracking and identification demonstration; first place in the capstone competition.

Workflow diagram

Key design decisions

Use agreement across multiple images

ChallengeOcclusion, turning and viewpoint changes make a single appearance comparison insufficient to link identities.

Approach and tradeoffsI designed track sampling, cross-image matching and dual thresholds for similarity and support. FastReID features and cosine similarity produce matches organized by the two conditions.

ResultTurned appearance matching into an adjustable, traceable decision process without claiming to eliminate occlusion or mistaken identity.

Connect ReID results back to tracking

ChallengeContinuous tracks require clear rules for when to match, when matching finishes and which record to update.

Approach and tradeoffsI managed pending track records with queues and states, connecting first and second checks, timeouts, completion and update states, and database handover.

ResultIntegrated ReID into the team workflow. The overall team delivered scenario demonstrations and won first place; real-time performance remained limited.
