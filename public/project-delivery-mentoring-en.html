Core problems and design decisions

Connect cross-service work to customer commitments

Tasks were scattered across repositories. Daily logs and individual branches did not show what the overall project still owed the customer.

I created tracking around customer commitments, linking rather than duplicating specifications and retaining human decisions. Milestones, open issues, task mappings and delivery checklists record agreement, disagreement and gaps from different roles for my decision.

Cross-repository progress, scope and decision ownership share one entry point. I remain responsible for external commitments and acceptance judgments.

Explore intern research offline; decide integration separately

Intern research can drift from required data prerequisites or move directly into a production product without review.

I defined product-based research problems, materials and weekly reports, with evaluations, recommendations and prototypes as outputs. I separated research from production-engine responsibilities, identified missing data prerequisites and topic drift, and required realignment. I assess formal integration separately.

Exploration and product integration are managed separately, with recorded direction, evaluation criteria and handover ownership. Interns retain research authorship.
