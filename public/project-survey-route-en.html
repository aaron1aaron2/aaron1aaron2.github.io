Core problems and design decisions

Constrain routes to each interviewer's real assignment

Sample addresses are dispersed. One shortest route across all areas may not reflect real work assignments.

Defined areas by district and interviewer ID, merged coordinates, built per-area distance matrices and used a route solver to produce visit sequences.

Produced total and average distances, sample sequences and maps per survey area for planning.

Keep planning possible when route distances are missing

Walking distances can be missing for many address pairs. Straight-line substitutes alone underestimate travel.

Retained straight-line baselines and estimated missing routes at 1.5 times that distance, storing retrieval results and completing both directions and self-distances for a usable matrix.

Kept planning from stopping at missing values. The coefficient was a historical rule, not a validated road-distance calibration.
