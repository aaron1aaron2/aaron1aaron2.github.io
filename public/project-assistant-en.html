Core problems and design decisions

Explain what the assistant is doing while users wait

Without concrete feedback during tool processing, users cannot distinguish waiting, retrieval and response states.

I added requirements and helped choose interactions and conduct device walkthroughs. I coordinated agents to connect waiting text, tool sources and visibility controls to actual processing states, including pushed status and citation handling.

Waiting feedback, tool information and citations are integrated into the platform, with records of my device walkthrough.

Trace leaked internal context back to knowledge sources

Answers to product questions exposed internal development modules. Hiding citations alone would not change the retrieval corpus.

I helped define external audience and content boundaries and coordinated AI agents to reorganize the corpus. Content without audience tags defaults to exclusion from the external knowledge base. The fix included reimporting content and clearing semantic caches to prevent stale answers.

The external corpus and audience safeguards are integrated and checked for retrieval, citations, leakage and failure fallback. Some full live-user Q&A scenarios remain unvalidated.

Results and validation scope

Integrated waiting feedback, tool information and citations, with a device walkthrough. The external knowledge base filters by audience tags, with retrieval and cache checks. Some full live-user Q&A scenarios remain unvalidated.

Scope and limitations

Interface, retrieval and cache behavior have been checked. Full user Q&A and live-data services continue to be validated by the responsible team members.
