Key problems and my approach

Withdraw a plausible AI conclusion and audit its evidence

AI had recommended a correction, but the cited real data was actually test constants and could not support a product decision.

I invalidated contaminated values and approvals, then required ablation across parameters and data shapes. I challenged data provenance and causality, comparing degradation and healthy sequences. Research notebooks called implementation functions directly and checked code hashes.

Controlled experiments exposed how an existing limit masked rapid deterioration and checked false alarms on healthy sequences, restoring a traceable basis for the correction.

Apply adoption policies before changing equipment thresholds

A new recommendation does not mean every sensor threshold should change immediately. Frequent changes make control hard to understand.

I built a threshold-feedback control layer separating calculation, adoption and dispatch, with manual policy and safety constraints. The platform checks global enablement, sensor policy, manual locks, cooldown and adjustment bounds, synchronizing values and history.

Recommendations, adoption and commands have separate accountability and history, while users retain manual control. Field lifetime ground truth still needs calibration.

Results and validation

Integrated threshold state and history synchronization, per-sensor automatic/manual policy and safety gates, checked through service and regression tests. Field lifetime accuracy remains uncalibrated.
