Key design decisions

Withdraw a plausible AI conclusion and audit its evidence

ChallengeAI had recommended a correction, but the cited real data was actually test constants and could not support a product decision.

Approach and tradeoffsI invalidated contaminated values and approvals, then required ablation across parameters and data shapes. I challenged data provenance and causality, comparing degradation and healthy sequences. Research notebooks called implementation functions directly and checked code hashes.

ResultControlled experiments exposed how an existing limit masked rapid deterioration and checked false alarms on healthy sequences, restoring a traceable basis for the correction.

Apply adoption policies before changing equipment thresholds

ChallengeA new recommendation does not mean every sensor threshold should change immediately. Frequent changes make control hard to understand.

Approach and tradeoffsI built a threshold-feedback control layer separating calculation, adoption and dispatch, with manual policy and safety constraints. The platform checks global enablement, sensor policy, manual locks, cooldown and adjustment bounds, synchronizing values and history.

ResultRecommendations, adoption and commands have separate accountability and history, while users retain manual control. Field lifetime ground truth still needs calibration.

Results and validation scope

Deliverables

Integrated threshold status and history synchronization, per-sensor policies for automatic adoption or manual settings, and safety limits. Service and regression tests were completed. Lifetime estimates still require calibration and validation against field data.

Validation and limits

Model comparisons and control-flow validation are complete; field lifetime ground truth and accuracy calibration are not established.
