Key design decisions

Give different readers the same evidence base

ChallengeManagers, developers and field engineers need different entry points. Storing documents alone does not explain current risks and status.

Approach and tradeoffsI contributed to documentation architecture and maintenance, turning changes, tests and risks into system, operation, manual and record navigation for different readers.

ResultSupports asynchronous work and handover by letting readers trace progress, risks and decisions to common evidence.

Share version references; verify each document against reality

ChallengeManuals, deployment documents, specifications and presentations serve different purposes. Copying one summary across them can detach them from the UI or deployment process.

Approach and tradeoffsI helped establish shared version references while checking manuals against the UI, installation documents against deployment, specifications against citations and presentations against audience questions.

ResultEstablished document-specific checks, separating version consistency from content correctness while retaining human review and drift tracking.
