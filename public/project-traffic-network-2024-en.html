Key design decisions

Turn metro structure into learnable tasks

ChallengeTransfer and regular stations differ; fixed network representations cannot fully reflect time-varying flow.

Approach and tradeoffsThe joint study used multiple pretraining tasks for station and spatial relationships, combining masked flow, station sequences and distance before transferring representations to a forecasting model.

ResultThe team completed dynamic transport methods and comparative experiments, producing a Knowledge-Based Systems article.

Combine time, nearby stations and routes hierarchically

ChallengeFlattening nearby stations can lose shared-route and transfer-direction structure.

Approach and tradeoffsThe joint study used MLAN to hierarchically integrate time, station and route signals, combining pretrained representations with GRU features and comparative validation.

ResultThe team developed comparable transport-network forecasting methods. My scope was code and technical support.
