Key design decisions

One station should not always have one fixed representation

ChallengeA fixed station vector cannot express different peak and off-peak conditions.

Approach and tradeoffsI handled method ideation, data processing, modeling and experiments. The joint research combined BERT pretraining with station distance and historical flow to generate time- and flow-dependent station representations for downstream estimation.

ResultTurned the idea into models and comparison experiments, producing a coauthored IIAI AAI 2021 paper and Competitive Paper Award.

Compare many experiments with limited training resources

ChallengeNeighborhood, distance and route conditions required separate comparisons, while limited GPUs and repeated preprocessing constrained experiments.

Approach and tradeoffsI ran model and parameter experiments, preserving configurations and results for different neighborhoods and representations. Across this and later research, I developed shell-based parallel runs, separate logs and models, H5 preprocessing caches and parameter exploration, used to different degrees in each project.

ResultRetained experiment histories for neighborhood and representation comparisons and result aggregation. These practices continued in later research, with project-specific use.
