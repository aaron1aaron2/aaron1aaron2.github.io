Passenger flow research (2021)

Study dynamic embeddings for passenger flow estimation.

My role
Handled method ideation, data processing, modeling and experiments for a coauthored study of dynamic embeddings and passenger flow estimation.
Record period
Published in 2021

The problem

Passenger flow estimation needs representations of time-varying transport relationships.

Inputs, process and outputs

Inputs
Station distances, historical flows and changing travel contexts.
Process
Pretrain dynamic representations of station relationships across time, then supply a downstream flow-estimation model.
Outputs
Method comparisons, models and experiments leading to an IIAI AAI paper and a team paper award.

Scope and limitations

Models and comparative experiments validate the method; the paper is coauthored.

Related cases

  • Dynamic transport networks and flow prediction (2024)

Key problems and my approach

One station should not always have one fixed representation

A fixed station vector cannot express different peak and off-peak conditions.

I handled method ideation, data processing, modeling and experiments. The joint research combined BERT pretraining with station distance and historical flow to generate time- and flow-dependent station representations for downstream estimation.

Turned the idea into models and comparison experiments, producing a coauthored IIAI AAI 2021 paper and Competitive Paper Award.

Compare many experiments with limited training resources

Neighborhood, distance and route conditions required separate comparisons, while limited GPUs and repeated preprocessing constrained experiments.

I ran model and parameter experiments, preserving configurations and results for different neighborhoods and representations. Across this and later research, I developed shell-based parallel runs, separate logs and models, H5 preprocessing caches and parameter exploration, used to different degrees in each project.

Retained experiment histories for neighborhood and representation comparisons and result aggregation. These practices continued in later research, with project-specific use.

Results and validation

Produced an IIAI AAI 2021 publication and Competitive Paper Award.
