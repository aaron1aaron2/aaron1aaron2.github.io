Joint smart-city cybersecurity proposal

Consolidate technical documents, versions, interfaces and review criteria for a joint proposal.

My role
Handle company-side technical documents, version integration and cross-company interfaces, turning control-plane, data-plane and device responsibilities into review criteria.
Record period
2026 proposal records

The problem

The joint proposal needed auditable responsibilities across control, data and device layers.

Inputs, process and outputs

Inputs
Joint concepts, technical requirements and partner responsibilities.
Process
Organize control, data and device interfaces, turning dependencies and responsibilities into checks and verifying references during revisions.
Outputs
Government-format proposal, presentation and traceable revision records.

Scope and limitations

Outputs are unsubmitted proposal and presentation drafts; development and deployment have not begun.

Key problems and my approach

Write cross-company responsibilities as auditable interfaces

The concept spanned control, data and device layers. Ambiguous ownership could leave interdependent checks and acceptance tasks unowned.

I aligned interfaces, dependencies and acceptance responsibilities with proposal checkpoints. I represented the company in document preparation and two alignment meetings, incorporating partner corrections into draft revisions.

Produced an auditable proposal structure and responsibility split for both parties' approval. Evidence covers proposal documents, not completed development or a contract award.

Make references and facts reviewable

Repeated drafts absorbed multiple sources, creating risks of reference, fact and table drift.

I established a formal reference style and recorded citation changes, fact corrections and repairs to prior defects, adding structure checks to version consolidation.

Created traceable revision notes and structural checks for changes to references, facts and responsibilities.

Results and validation

Completed a government-format proposal and presentation with clarified interfaces and checkpoints. They remain unsubmitted drafts.
