Key design decisions

Write cross-company responsibilities as auditable interfaces

ChallengeThe concept spanned control, data and device layers. Ambiguous ownership could leave interdependent checks and acceptance tasks unowned.

Approach and tradeoffsI aligned interfaces, dependencies and acceptance responsibilities with proposal checkpoints. I represented the company in document preparation and two alignment meetings, incorporating partner corrections into draft revisions.

ResultProduced an auditable proposal structure and responsibility split for both parties' approval. Evidence covers proposal documents, not completed development or a contract award.

Make references and facts reviewable

ChallengeRepeated drafts absorbed multiple sources, creating risks of reference, fact and table drift.

Approach and tradeoffsI established a formal reference style and recorded citation changes, fact corrections and repairs to prior defects, adding structure checks to version consolidation.

ResultCreated traceable revision notes and structural checks for changes to references, facts and responsibilities.
