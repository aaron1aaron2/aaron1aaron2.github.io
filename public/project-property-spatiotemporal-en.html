Core problems and design decisions

Define what the price of a piece of land means

Sparse, irregular transactions must become comparable regional time-series targets.

I proposed the direction, coordinated the team and mainly implemented models and experiments. The team defined monthly average unit prices and transaction volumes within 3,000 meters of reference land points, organizing registered transactions and regions into a spatiotemporal task.

Turned the competition topic into two trainable, explainable tasks. Targets are regional averages, distinct from valuing individual properties.

Turn model experiments into a clear final presentation

Data conditions vary across regions, and one aggregate score can hide errors.

I ran experiments and prepared the final presentation, organizing MAE, RMSE and MAPE by region and retaining settings and reproduction steps. Cached preprocessing and parallel experiments helped manage limited resources; I presented results with their limitations.

Completed models, experiments and final delivery. The three-person team received the Creativity Award; regional differences remained visible rather than hidden in one score.
