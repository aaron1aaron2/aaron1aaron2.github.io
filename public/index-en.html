Horus · Multi-camera person identification

The team combined multi-camera tracking, person ReID and face recognition to reconnect records after tracks break.

Context
A 2020 Soochow University capstone on multi-camera person tracking and identification.
Core problem

Challenges
Single images are sensitive to viewpoint and occlusion; matches also need to reconnect with continuously updated tracking records.
