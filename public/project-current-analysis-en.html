Equipment current data review and explanation

Revisited statistics and interpretation behind disputed analysis and prepared a revised customer explanation.

My role
Turned three customer observations into testable questions. Used minute medians, nonparametric tests, multiple-comparison correction and effect sizes to complete the review and customer explanation.
Record period
2026 analysis records

The problem

Customer questions about load and steady-state vibration required consistent statistics and a distinction between association and cause.

Inputs, process and outputs

Inputs
Customer observations, prior reports and completed equipment measurement data.
Process
Align coverage and event definitions, then compare operating conditions using minute medians, nonparametric tests and effect sizes.
Outputs
Statistical analysis, customer explanations and hypotheses requiring field verification.

Scope and limitations

Statistical differences test customer observations; root-cause attribution still requires field conditions such as speed and flow.

Key problems and my approach

Turn customer concerns into testable questions

After reading the vibration report, the customer raised observations about load, direction and steady-state vibration that the aggregate account could not answer.

I stratified by operating condition and separated statistical associations from unconfirmed equipment causes. Minute medians reduced second-level autocorrelation; multiple-comparison correction and effect sizes supported re-examination of the three observations and the customer explanation.

Delivered the detailed analysis and explanation, separating association, supported interpretation and hypotheses needing field verification.

Fill data gaps before redefining events

Gaps on the calendar can misclassify running equipment as stopped, changing event segmentation and statistics.

The analysis records separately checked two data pipelines, filled gaps, aligned overlapping timestamps and resegmented events. I revised the analytical definitions and customer explanation against the updated data.

Resegmented events and updated downstream references so conclusions share a consistent data baseline. The cause of node interruptions remains a separate investigation.

Results and validation

Delivered the analysis and customer presentation, separating statistical differences from causal hypotheses and listing information needed from the field.
