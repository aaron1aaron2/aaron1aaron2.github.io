Key design decisions

Turn customer concerns into testable questions

ChallengeAfter reading the vibration report, the customer raised observations about load, direction and steady-state vibration that the aggregate account could not answer.

Approach and tradeoffsI stratified by operating condition and separated statistical associations from unconfirmed equipment causes. Minute medians reduced second-level autocorrelation; multiple-comparison correction and effect sizes supported re-examination of the three observations and the customer explanation.

ResultDelivered the detailed analysis and explanation, separating association, supported interpretation and hypotheses needing field verification.

Fill data gaps before redefining events

ChallengeGaps on the calendar can misclassify running equipment as stopped, changing event segmentation and statistics.

Approach and tradeoffsThe analysis records separately checked two data pipelines, filled gaps, aligned overlapping timestamps and resegmented events. I revised the analytical definitions and customer explanation against the updated data.

ResultResegmented events and updated downstream references so conclusions share a consistent data baseline. The cause of node interruptions remains a separate investigation.
