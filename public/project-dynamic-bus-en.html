Context and objectives

Architecture overview

Inputs

Outputs

Key design decisions

Convert monthly tourism totals into scheduling signals

ChallengeMonthly visit totals do not provide the weekday and hourly shapes needed for timetables.

Approach and tradeoffsCombined search interest and popular times to allocate monthly visits across weekdays and hours, connecting tourism data, Google Trends and Popular Times.

ResultProduced time-based demand inputs for route and timetable exploration; these are not measured ridership.

Make routes and timing understandable

ChallengeDistance matrices and solver output alone do not make the relationship between routes and visitor flows easy to discuss.

Approach and tradeoffsUsed maps, timetables and flow diagrams for different aspects of the problem. Built distance matrices per group, solved TSP and produced Folium, Gantt and Sankey exploration views.

ResultCompleted an exploratory workflow from demand inputs to route visualization. There is no evidence of operational adoption or cost savings.

Deliverables

Validation and limits
