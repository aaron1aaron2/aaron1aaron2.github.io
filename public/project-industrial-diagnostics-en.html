Key design decisions

Pattern, severity and lifetime answer different questions

ChallengeA single health score combining fault class, vibration level and lifetime makes it unclear whether a value represents cause, degree or time.

Approach and tradeoffsI led a layered sequence from equipment information and preprocessing through operating detection, classification, ISO assessment, RUL and reporting, retaining each layer's data and confidence.

ResultEstablished an interpretable, traceable workflow that limits or stops inference when evidence is insufficient and states the supported level of conclusion.

Ask what the anomaly percentage divides by

ChallengeTaking only an extreme value per operating segment is easy, but gives short and long segments equal weight instead of representing exposure time fairly.

Approach and tradeoffsI responded to stakeholder challenges with time-bin comparisons while acknowledging incomplete raw-waveform coverage. I separated event, time and observed-waveform ratios and compared bias, coverage and system costs.

ResultBrought statistical definitions and production-data limits into decisions. Time-bin sampling remains research; final algorithm selection is not claimed.
