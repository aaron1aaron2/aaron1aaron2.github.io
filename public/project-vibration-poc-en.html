Key design decisions

Define supported inference before generating a report

ChallengeCoverage and operating proportions vary. Always emitting a class and lifetime would create certainty unsupported by the data.

Approach and tradeoffsI led the diagnostic chain and quality boundaries, making data sufficiency, operating coverage and fit confidence visible fallback conditions. AI agents and skills supported data processing and traceable report artifacts.

ResultBuilt a workflow from equipment and data facts through diagnosis, severity and time, retaining limitations instead of forcing conclusions.

Leave reusable methods after a one-off analysis

ChallengeCustomer analysis spans metadata, preprocessing and multiple diagnostic stages. Ad hoc conversations alone do not support reruns or consistent review.

Approach and tradeoffsValidated quality rules in reusable analytical steps and turned them into a workflow. I led process design and interpretation rules, verifying partial reruns, incomplete-data handling, and intermediate results before integrating the report service.

ResultRetained a path from proof of concept to reusable reporting. Later service integration is documented in the reporting project.
