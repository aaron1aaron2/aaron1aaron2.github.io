Core problems and design decisions

Define supported inference before generating a report

Coverage and operating proportions vary. Always emitting a class and lifetime would create certainty unsupported by the data.

I led the diagnostic chain and quality boundaries, making data sufficiency, operating coverage and fit confidence visible fallback conditions. AI agents and skills supported data processing and traceable report artifacts.

Built a workflow from equipment and data facts through diagnosis, severity and time, retaining limitations instead of forcing conclusions.

Leave reusable methods after a one-off analysis

Customer analysis spans metadata, preprocessing and multiple diagnostic stages. Ad hoc conversations alone do not support reruns or consistent review.

I led workflow and interpretation definitions, iterating with skills before converting stable node responsibilities and quality rules into orchestration. Agents and engineers helped validate partial reruns, data fallback and artifacts, leading toward report services.

Retained a path from proof of concept to reusable reporting. Later service integration is documented in the reporting project.
