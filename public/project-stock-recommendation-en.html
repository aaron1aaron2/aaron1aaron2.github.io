Key design decisions

Bring separate recommendation modules into one usable interface

ChallengeDifferent members handled recommendation and information modules; separate results did not give users a complete flow from recommendations to stock queries.

Approach and tradeoffsI proposed the topic and direction, organized responsibilities and mainly built and deployed the Telegram chatbot, integrating teammates' recommendation and information modules into one conversational entry point.

ResultThe team delivered an integrated recommendation and stock-information chat system, receiving the course competition's Excellence Award. My focus was turning separate outputs into a usable system.

Choose recommendations that suit the data

ChallengeFinancial features and user behavior have different limitations. Weak clustering and sparse behavior required reconsidering available methods.

Approach and tradeoffsI suggested algorithm ideas and integrated team methods into the chatbot. The team explored company features, similar users and stock co-occurrence, discarding poorly performing KMeans. Method choices retain team attribution.

ResultCompleted an explainable recommendation prototype connected to chat and received the course competition's Excellence Award. Online clicks and trading outcomes were not measured.
