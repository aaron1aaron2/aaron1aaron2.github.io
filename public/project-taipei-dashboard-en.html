Core problems and design decisions

Ask who resources serve, beyond facility counts

Facility counts alone cannot compare population needs with resources around tourism hotspots.

I handled the concept, specifications and presentation, turning population, attractions and disaster resources into discussable requirements and aligning the proposal direction.

Defined team analysis directions for district allocation, shelter capacity and facilities near attractions, completing the proposal and reaching the final.

Separate district allocation from attraction-level needs

Population, shelter capacity and attraction locations use different spatial scales. One totals table cannot answer both district allocation and nearby-resource questions.

I organized proposal needs and perspectives into specifications and delivered the presentation. The team handled data and visualization for district population/resources, shelter capacity and facilities near attractions.

Readers can inspect district allocation and attraction surroundings separately, with an appropriate view for each question.
